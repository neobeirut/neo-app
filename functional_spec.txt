Flow ERPFunctional SpecificationSupplier Price Intelligence Module

Version: 1.0Purpose: Build a supplier intelligence module that analyzes historical purchasing and receiving data, compares supplier quotations, monitors price fluctuations, evaluates supplier performance, and provides AI-driven purchasing recommendations.

Objectives

Compare supplier prices.

Monitor historical price fluctuations.

Identify savings opportunities.

Evaluate suppliers by price, quality, reliability, payment terms and delivery.

Import supplier price lists manually, Excel/CSV, and AI OCR from PDFs/images.

Generate explainable AI purchasing recommendations.

Integration

Reuse existing Purchasing, Receiving, Inventory, Item Catalog, Supplier, Dashboard and Financial Analytics modules.

Do not duplicate purchasing data.

Dashboard

Purchasing KPIs, supplier rankings, price alerts, savings opportunities, expired quotations, abnormal price changes.

Supplier Management

Supplier profile, payment terms, lead time, preferred supplier, performance indicators, quality and reliability ratings.

Item Price Intelligence

Current, lowest, highest and weighted average price.

Historical graphs.

Monthly consumption.

Estimated savings.

Best supplier.

Supplier Comparison

Compare suppliers using normalized prices, quality, reliability, payment terms, lead time, MOQ and Best Value Score.

Price List Management

Manual entry.

Excel/CSV import.

PDF/Image OCR import using AI.

Effective and expiry dates.

Currency, taxes and delivery inclusion.

AI Item Matching

Match supplier item descriptions to Flow items.

Require user approval for new mappings.

Store approved mappings.

Unit Normalization

Normalize Kg, Gram, Liter, ml, Piece, Pack, Box, Carton, Bottle and Roll before comparison.

Calculations

Lowest price.

Highest price.

Weighted average.

Price increase/decrease percentages.

Price volatility.

Estimated monthly and annual savings.

Supplier Evaluation

Rate quality, freshness, packaging, specification compliance, delivery, communication, service and replacement handling (1–5).

Supplier Performance

On-time delivery.

Rejected deliveries.

Returns.

Invoice variance.

Lead time.

Order fulfilment.

Best Value Algorithm

Default weights: Price 40%, Quality 25%, Reliability 15%, Payment Terms 10%, Delivery 5%, Service 5%.

Administrator configurable.

AI Purchasing Assistant

Answer procurement questions.

Recommend suppliers with clear reasoning.

Prepare negotiation summaries.

Identify negotiation opportunities.

Price Alerts

Price increases/decreases.

Expired quotations.

Invoice differs from quotation.

Better supplier available.

Reports

Supplier comparison.

Price history.

Supplier performance.

Savings.

Negotiation report.

Permissions

Administrator, Purchasing Manager, Store Manager (view), Finance.

Future Enhancements

Supplier portal.

Automatic RFQs.

Market price APIs.

Demand forecasting.

Supplier risk analysis.

Business Rules

Never recommend a supplier based only on price.

Always normalize units.

Explain every AI recommendation.

Keep complete audit history.

Reuse existing purchasing data.

Implementation Guidance for Antigravity

Design the database schema first, followed by APIs/business logic, then the user interface, and finally the AI layer. If a business requirement cannot be implemented exactly as specified, propose an alternative before changing the functional behavior.
